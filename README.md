# Can Lorenzo Thrive in the Philippine Economy?

## Project Overview

Lorenzo is a proposed Philippine luxury and performance automotive company.

This data analytics project investigates whether Lorenzo could become a viable and sustainable automotive company in the Philippine market based on economic conditions, automotive demand, consumer purchasing power, competitor pricing, and industry trends.

## Research Question

**Can Lorenzo thrive as a Philippine luxury and performance automotive company given the current and future conditions of the Philippine economy and automotive market?**

## Objectives

- Analyze the growth of the Philippine automotive industry
- Examine Philippine GDP, inflation, household income, and purchasing power
- Study demand for premium and luxury vehicles
- Analyze the growth of hybrid, PHEV, and electric vehicles
- Compare Lorenzo's proposed pricing with existing competitors
- Estimate Lorenzo's potential addressable market
- Identify risks and opportunities for launching a Filipino luxury automotive brand

## Analysis Approach

The project will use:

- Descriptive Analysis
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Competitive Analysis
- Market Segmentation
- Predictive Analysis
- Scenario Analysis
